UnLate: Autonomous Skateboard

Swift iOS App & Edge AI

Problem

Skateboards have existed forever, so why not make them drive themselves? Cars have been driving themselves for years, but why hasn't a solution for single person autonomous transportation been developed?

Solution

Pair ESP32-CAM edge sensing with on-device CoreML inference on iOS and a custom BLDC powertrain with rack-and-pinion steering.

Engineering

  • ESP32-CAM + custom C firmware for low-latency video streaming.
  • Swift app running YOLOv5L through CoreML for on-device inference.
  • CV pipeline translating detections into real-time motor and steering commands.
  • Dual 6384 BLDC motors for rear-wheel drive belt actuation with torque control.
  • Custom spot welded, soldered, and developed 12S3P pack (44.4V, ~540 Wh) with integrated BMS.
  • CNC 6061 aluminum trucks with rack-and-pinion front steering via planetary geared servo.
ESP32-CAM CoreML YOLOv5 BLDC CNC Swift
September 2025 – Present

NMHS Electronic Shelf Labeling

E-Ink Digital Shelf Labels · YOLO OCR

Problem

Stores spend dozens of labor hours per week swapping paper shelf tags, especially as price update frequency increases.

Most digital shelf label systems force retailers to abandon existing tagging PDF workflows and overhaul store processes.

Solution

E-ink shelf labels with a PDF digitization pipeline, YOLO OCR to read text straight off existing tags, and WiFi distribution to on-chip firmware on each label.

Engineering

  • Digitalization pipeline ingests existing retailer tagging PDFs as the source layout for each shelf tag.
  • YOLO used as OCR: detects text regions on each tag and extracts price, product name, and field data without manual re-entry.
  • Store-wide updates over WiFi to on-chip firmware on each label for local rendering.
  • Light-powered e-ink displays run on ambient indoor lighting with no battery replacements.
  • Quick Clip front panel for promo cards, barcodes, and discounts without replacing the tag.
  • Shelf-integrated hardware designed for mass production; saves 10+ labor hours/week at ~50% competitor pricing.
E-Ink YOLO OCR WiFi IoT
